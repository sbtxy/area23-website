Who We Are

Area 23 serves Manassas, Manassas Park, and Prince William County as part of Special Olympics Virginia. We offer year-round sports training and competition for people with intellectual disabilities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Special Olympics Virginia athlete registration. Select a question to learn more.

1. Who needs to complete the Athlete Registration Form?
Athletes with intellectual disabilities must complete or update their registration form annually.
2. Who can complete the form?
Athletes aged 18 or older who can legally sign documents may complete their own forms. Otherwise, a parent or legal guardian with signing authority must complete the form.
3. How do I access the Athlete Registration Portal?
SOVA sends an invitation email from athletes@specialolympicsva.org to create an account. Each account needs a unique username.
4. Can I register multiple athletes using the same email address?
Yes. The same email address can be used, but each athlete needs a unique username formatted like an email address.
5. Who do I contact if I have trouble creating an account?
Email athletes@specialolympicsva.org for help with usernames or logging in.
6. How long does registration processing take?
SOVA receives submissions immediately. Completing the athlete profile may take up to 48 business hours.
7. How do I know when registration needs renewal?
The athlete and parent or guardian on file receive an email 30 days before the registration form expires.
8. What are the benefits of using the portal?
Previously entered information is prefilled for future renewals, and athletes can update their information when necessary.
9. What if contact or medical information changes?
You can update the registration form at any time. Submitting an update resets the eligibility date to one year after the updated submission.
10. Are paper registration forms accepted?
SOVA strongly discourages paper and PDF forms and directs applicants to the online portal.

These answers summarize SOVA registration guidance. Check the official page for the latest details.
